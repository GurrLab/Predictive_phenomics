### About

Experiments were completed in May 2026 with NOAA Northeast Oyster Breeding Center (NOBC). Oyster larvae were collected from sieved CUDL tanks targeting the most abundant families, and those furthest in development toward ~250-300 um. Nine families were assigned by the hatchery lead. Daily, samples were transported from the NOAA Milford Laboratory to the Yale Chemical Metabolism Core for trials 

#### Content 

* **Seahorse**, Two successful runs were completed at 25 and 34C. 
  *  25C trial was loaded as 1-2 oysters per well. Data is limited in this trial because many wells at baseline were below LOD.
  * 34C trail was loaded as 5 oysters per well. After learning from the 25C trial (low LOD), the greater target density per well yielded better data. 
  * AA/Rot normalization 
    * Given the differences in stocking density between temperatures, we use a different method than normalizations on count from bright field images. We normalized based on the assumed shut down of the mito. by inhibiting both complex III and complex 1 with antimycin A and rotenone respectively, thereby halting electron transport. Importantly, this does assume the non-mitochondrial respiration is the same at both temperatures. 

* **Oroboros**. One successful trial run at ambient temperature
